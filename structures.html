How to use this in an interview

When asked “what would you recommend?”, map the problem to the matrix: who needs liquidity (LP, GP, fund, company), how much upside is being sold, at what cost of capital, over what timeline, and how much conflict the structure imports. The ILPA 2026 draft now expects GPs to justify a CV explicitly against these alternatives — so the matrix is also the GP’s homework.
