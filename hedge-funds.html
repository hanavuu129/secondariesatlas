The vocabulary that gets tested

Gross exposure = Longs + |Shorts| (e.g., 150% long + 100% short = 250% gross) Net exposure = Longs − |Shorts| (same book = 50% net long) Alpha = return unexplained by market beta; Beta = sensitivity to the market Sharpe = (Return − Rf) / Volatility; Max drawdown = peak-to-trough loss
  • Short mechanics: borrow shares, sell, repurchase later. Costs: borrow fee, dividends owed. Risks: unlimited loss, squeezes, recalls.
  • A pitch is not a report: the format is thesis → variant perception (what the market misses) → catalysts → valuation with upside/downside → risks and sizing. Every stock pitch you give must have a "why does this mispricing exist" answer.
  • Multi-manager economics: pass-through expenses instead of flat fees; PMs get cut when they hit drawdown limits (~5%); ferocious but meritocratic. Single-manager funds offer longer duration ideas and more concentration.
