The one-sentence distinction

PE makes money by changing companies over years (operations, capital structure, strategy). Hedge funds make money by being right before the market is (mispricing, catalysts, flows). The skill sets, lifestyles, and interviews are correspondingly different.

Where the money comes from

Both are funded by limited partners (LPs): pensions, sovereign wealth funds, endowments, insurance companies, funds-of-funds, and family offices. PE locks capital in ~10-year fund structures; hedge funds hold it in evergreen vehicles with periodic redemption rights. Understanding LPs matters because they set the incentives — fee pressure, DPI demands, and liquidity needs drive almost everything discussed below (including the entire existence of the secondaries market).
