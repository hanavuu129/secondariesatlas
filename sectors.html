The universal diligence framework (direct-PE grade, with a secondary overlay)

Commercial

Market size and growth, competitive position, pricing power, customer concentration, churn and retention, win/loss analysis, channel checks and expert calls.

Financial & QoE

Quality of earnings and add-backs, revenue recognition, cash conversion, working capital normalization, capex classification, pro forma adjustments on add-ons, management-case vs. sponsor-case bridge.

Operational

Management depth and succession, KPIs by business unit, systems and data quality, integration track record, procurement and supply chain, operating-partner levers.

Legal & regulatory

Material contracts and change-of-control, litigation, licenses and permits, antitrust posture, data privacy, employment matters, regulatory calendar over the hold.

Capital structure

Leverage and covenant headroom, maturities and refinancing risk, PIK and toggle features, intercreditor dynamics, hedging, dividend-recap history.

ESG & reputation

Environmental liabilities, safety record, labor practices, reputational tail risks that become exit obstacles.

Tax & structure

Entity structure and blockers, NOL usage, transfer taxes, withholding (1446(f)), management rollover and incentive plan mechanics.

Secondary-specific overlay

GP alignment (carry roll, new money, vesting), reference-date staleness, mark credibility vs. comps, process integrity (real competition?), exit path realism (who buys next, and is it another CV?), fund-level terms (fees, carry tiers, governance, follow-on capacity).

Using this in a case

Open with the universal framework, then pull the sector’s KPIs and the secondary lens. The sentence that wins: “For a [sector] single-asset CV I’d anchor on [two KPIs], test the mark against [comps], and my standard downside is [sector shock].”
