How sponsors underwrite a deal

  1. Sourcing: banked auctions vs. proprietary angles. Auction stages: teaser → NDA → CIM → first-round IOIs → management presentations → data room / QoE → final binding bids → exclusivity.
  2. Diligence workstreams: commercial (market study), financial (quality of earnings), legal, tax/structure, insurance, ESG, technology.
  3. The IC memo: thesis, base/downside cases, value-creation plan, exit paths, "what has to be true."
  4. The 100-day plan: management assessment, quick pricing wins, procurement, KPI dashboards, add-on pipeline.
  5. Exit paths: strategic sale, sponsor-to-sponsor, IPO, dividend recap (partial), or — increasingly — a continuation vehicle (Section VI).
Case study framing

When handed "should we buy this company?" — structure the answer: (1) What do we pay and how is it funded? (2) What's the value-creation plan (growth, margins, M&A)? (3) What do we exit at, to whom, and when? (4) What breaks the deal (downside case)? Land on a number and a recommendation. Fence-sitting scores worse than a defended wrong answer.
