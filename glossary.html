GLOSSARY

Glossary

Add-on acquisition
Smaller acquisition bolted onto an existing platform company, usually at a lower multiple.
Bid-ask spread (secondaries)
Gap between what sellers want (near NAV) and buyers will pay; widens when marks are doubted.
Capital call
GP's demand that LPs fund part of their commitment, typically on ~10 days' notice.
Carried interest
GP's share of profits (typically 20%) above the preferred return.
Cash sweep
Mandatory use of excess free cash flow to prepay debt in an LBO.
CIM
Confidential information memorandum — the sell-side marketing document in an M&A process.
Clawback
Provision requiring the GP to return excess carry if later deals underperform (deal-by-deal waterfalls).
Continuation vehicle (CV)
New fund formed by a GP to acquire assets from its own existing fund, giving old LPs a sell-or-roll choice.
Covenant-lite
Loans without maintenance covenants; lender protection only triggers on incurrence events.
Denominator effect
Public market declines mechanically push LPs over their private-markets allocation targets, forcing secondary sales.
DPI
Distributions to paid-in capital — the realized-return multiple LPs increasingly prioritize.
Dry powder
Committed but un-deployed capital.
Fairness opinion
Third-party opinion that a transaction price is fair — common in GP-led deals given the conflict.
Fulcrum security
The tranche in a distressed capital structure that converts to equity control in restructuring.
GP commit
The GP's own capital invested in its fund (often 1–5%) for alignment.
Gross vs. net exposure
Gross = longs + |shorts| (leverage); net = longs − |shorts| (market directionality).
Hurdle rate / preferred return
Minimum return (typically 8%) LPs must receive before carry is paid.
ILPA
Institutional Limited Partners Association — publishes LP-friendly guidance, including on GP-led processes.
IOI / LOI
Indication of interest (first-round, non-binding) / letter of intent (later-stage, exclusivity terms).
J-curve
Pattern of negative early fund returns (fees, cost-held assets) turning positive later.
Leakage
Value leaving a target between a locked-box reference date and closing (dividends, fees to seller).
LPAC
LP advisory committee — approves conflicts, valuations, extensions on behalf of the LP base.
Management fee offset
Reduction of fund management fees by portfolio-company fees the GP collects.
MOIC
Multiple on invested capital — total value / invested, ignoring time.
NAV loan
Facility secured against a fund's portfolio NAV, used for follow-ons or accelerating distributions — controversial when it layers leverage LPs didn't expect.
PIK (payment-in-kind)
Interest that accrues to principal rather than paying cash; compounds against the equity at exit.
Quality of earnings (QoE)
Accounting diligence report validating and adjusting EBITDA (one-timers, run-rate items, pro formas).
Recycling
Provision letting a GP reinvest early proceeds instead of distributing them.
Rollover equity
Seller/management equity reinvested into the new deal — an alignment signal buyers scrutinize.
Sharpe ratio
Excess return per unit of volatility — the standard risk-adjusted return measure.
Strip sale
Sale of a proportional slice of several fund assets into a new vehicle, rather than whole positions.
Subscription (capital call) facility
Fund-level credit line secured by LP commitments; bridges capital calls and flatters IRR.
Tender offer (GP-led)
Process where a secondary buyer offers to purchase stakes from any willing LPs in a fund at a set price.
TSA
Transition services agreement — parent-provided services (IT, payroll) supporting a carve-out post-close.
TVPI
Total value (distributions + NAV) to paid-in capital.
Unfunded commitment
Capital an LP has promised but not yet been called — transfers with the interest in a secondary sale.
Vintage year
The year a fund begins investing; the peer group against which its returns are judged.
Waterfall
The contractual order of distributions between LPs and GP (return of capital → pref → catch-up → split).
